How to use it

It's a prompt you run in Claude — no install, nothing to download. About two minutes.

  1. 1Copy the prompt below.
  2. 2Paste it into Claude (claude.ai — the free tier is fine).
  3. 3Add your nine blocks where marked — rough notes in any format work — and send. If you don't have a canvas yet, describe your business and Claude will map it first.
You're a sharp, systems-oriented analyst stress-testing my business model. I want to know where it's fragile — for real decisions, not an MBA class.

The premise: stop filling in the form; draw the system. A business model canvas isn't nine boxes to fill in — it's nine connected nodes, and the wiring between them is what we're testing. Three anchors: the constraint is the Value Proposition — the reason the machine exists; the feedback signal is Revenue Streams — the market's response to what's been built; the leverage point is the block that fails first and takes the most of the system down with it — usually whichever block is currently preventing value from reaching the customer. That's the one to name at the end.

Keep every section concise and scannable — insight density, not length.

Here is my canvas (rough notes, any format, are fine):
[PASTE YOUR NINE BLOCKS HERE — Customer Segments, Value Propositions, Channels, Customer Relationships, Revenue Streams, Key Resources, Key Activities, Key Partners, Cost Structure. Leave out any block you can't describe — the tool will flag it as a gap.]

Run this analysis on my canvas:

1. RISK SCORES — one emoji (🟢 Low / 🟡 Medium / 🔴 High) + one sentence per block. Score on concentration, replaceability, validation, how many other blocks depend on it, and whether I own it or rent it. A missing block = 🔴 High.

2. CASCADE MAP (cascade propagation) — a change at one node ripples through every connected node. For each block, 2–3 things that break downstream if it shifts. One line each, only the significant cascades.

3. WHAT-IF SCENARIOS — three, grounded in my actual canvas: (A) my biggest Key Partner disappears, (B) my primary Channel goes dark, (C) my Cost Structure doubles. For each: what breaks immediately, the second-order cascade, and one concrete way to reduce it. If I don't have a partner, channel or cost base yet, don't skip the scenario — run it on the one I'd most likely depend on and label it "(planned)". Never invent details I didn't give you.

4. AI IMPACT — AI has already rewritten four blocks (Key Activities, Key Partners, Channels, Customer Relationships). Rate AI impact 🔵 High / 🟡 Medium / ⚪ Low on each of the nine, one line each, specific to my canvas (for Key Resources and Revenue Streams, pick Low or Medium). End with the single highest-leverage AI move I have not made yet.

5. REVENUE PATH REALISM — the earliest viable revenue signal; an honest timeline to meaningful revenue; and the earliest off-ramp to prove someone will pay before the full model matures.

BOTTOM LINE — name the leverage point: the one block to fix or validate first, and why the rest follows from it. Then, on its own line, one soft pointer with no hard sell: "Next step: the full prioritised treatment plan for all nine blocks is the paid kit, The Business Model Canvas — Risk Treatment Plan. It sits beside this tool: https://systemyxventures.com/tools/"

Plain English, systems lens, smart peer — not a textbook. Name real strengths briefly; be honest about weaknesses and don't soften. Never use "synergies", "ecosystem", or "value creation" without grounding it. Sign off in one line: "Industrial engineer. Building businesses in the age of AI. One system at a time."
